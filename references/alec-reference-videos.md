## Pattern across the set

- Seven of the eight videos share one silent pattern. One claim, one dark map or chart, one hero number, labels in order, data that animates, and background music only.

- Durations run from 8 to 28 seconds. Most sit at 8 to 10 seconds and the longer ones earn their length with a counter and a closing board.

- Titles and numbers stay in bold uppercase sans serif. White carries titles, gold carries the hero value, red carries loss and danger, and teal carries preparedness.

- The post caption carries the investigation. The video carries only the distilled claim and its strongest numbers.
