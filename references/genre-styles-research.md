# Genre styles research, 30 October 2026

Researched on social platforms and YouTube at the user request to grow beyond the Alec data set. Each entry keeps the production mechanics, not just the name. Styles marked ORIGINAL FUSION are combinations created for this skill rather than copies of one channel.

## Storytelling

- Animated map history (Chronomap, MapTale, Kings and Generals): one map fills the frame for the whole video, territory colour floods across borders like liquid, a large year counter never leaves its corner and ticks faster in wartime, flags pop at capitals, music only in timelapse form. Suspense comes from watching a colour win or lose like a sport.
- Chat thread story (texting story format): a pixel faithful phone chat fills the frame, bubbles pop in one at a time and push the stack up, the typing dots are the core suspense device, timestamp jumps signal escalation, the last bubble hangs alone. Viewers are conditioned by real life to finish a thread.
- Case file documentary (true crime shorts): an evidence montage of photos, clippings and map shots, desaturated and dark graded, every still gets a slow push in, fact stamps slam in the centre third, and a rigid four beat structure runs hook, setup, climax, solved or unsolved status in about sixty seconds.
- Withheld reveal narration (MrBallen model): a plain visual field and one expressive voice, the story told out of order from its strangest detail, key facts openly withheld until the last tenth. Proof that editing structure alone can be the style when the host carries it.

## Economics and business

- Bar chart race (popularised by John Burn-Murdoch): five to ten bars with fixed colours swap places smoothly as values change, the overtake is the plot twist, a giant date counter sits in a corner, music only, and viewers pick a horse and stay to see it win.
- Money flow Sankey (follow the dollar): sources on the left fan into expense bands on the right, band width equals amount, bands grow one at a time in narration order, amounts travel along the bands as moving labels, and the full diagram holds at the end for screenshots.
- Rise and fall mini movie (MagnatesMedia): a company treated as a film protagonist in three acts, the opening implies the fall, the story rewinds, a price line climbs in the rise act and falls off a cliff at the collapse beat, money figures land at each turning point.
- Data poster short (WSJ and Bloomberg QuickTake): one chart or one number fills the frame, a single accent colour, the chart draws itself in sync with the voice, one segment highlights while the rest greys out, and a verdict word slams in at the end.

## News

- Paper collage explainer (Vox): an aged newsprint tabletop stage, black and white halftone cutouts with white keylines spring in with overshoot, one hot red is reserved for underlines and arrows, labels type on like pulled strips, paper shuffle and stamp sounds sync to entries, and nothing ever fades because paper does not fade.
- Election tracker (NYT Needle, AP): three locked layers, a choropleth that flips colour as regions are called, a seat bar growing toward a fixed majority threshold line, and a gauge needle that jitters inside its uncertainty band before it settles. Batch updates and the threshold crossing are the drama.
- Satellite before and after (NYT Visual Investigations, Bellingcat): one full frame image pair in perfect registration, a wipe line drags the after image across the before, footprint outlines fill red one by one as damage is counted, counts and dates are stamped on the image itself, and the sound stays near silent so the proof carries it.
- Synced timeline reconstruction (Washington Post Visual Forensics): a running clock never leaves the frame, unsynced clips are re-synced to one master timeline by waveform spikes, a map or floor plan advances its dots with the clock, and freeze frames with outlines mark the decisive second.

## Horror

- Analog horror (Local 58): a fake public access broadcast in low resolution, station cards and schedule listings with one wrong entry, signal decay as the only motion, glitch bursts timed exactly to the scary line, tape hiss, detuned synths and long near silence. The trusted boring format is corrupted from inside.
- EAS scenario: pixel faithful emergency alert screens, scrolling text crawls at authentic speed, severity banners escalate, then a second unauthorised alert hijacks the first with contradictory instructions. The real alert tone is a conditioned fear stimulus and authority itself becomes unreliable.
- Creepypasta narration shorts: one dark illustrated world per channel, slow camera creep on stills, word by word captions, half a second of total silence before the scare line, and a final line that recontextualises everything before a cut to black with no explanation.
- Photo dossier horror (The Mandela Catalogue): fake official archives and classification slides, grainy photographs with subtly warped faces, an almost imperceptible slow zoom, and one blink change in the image that viewers argue about in the comments.
- Ghost story radio (Vietnamese truyen ma radio): an audio first format with one painterly night illustration, fog and rain overlays, a signature low slow narrator, folk subjects such as grave rituals and karmic retribution, and the ritual instruction to listen with headphones in the dark. Imagination does the rendering.
- Kaidan and imi-kowa (Japanese tradition): candlelit telling from the hyaku monogatari ritual of one hundred tales with one candle extinguished per story, and imi-kowa stories whose scare detonates after the video ends because the final line reframes the first one, which manufactures rewatches.

## Original fusions created for this skill

- Hundred Candles: the hyaku monogatari candle ritual crossed with the counter craft of the data styles. A grid of candles fills the dark frame and one candle dies per story beat with a smoke wisp, so the light itself is the progress bar and the frame is darkest exactly when the story is.
- Alert Hijack as a data format: the EAS hijack mechanic applied to real warning and market alert content, where a calm first alert is overridden by a second contradictory one, used only with clearly fictional or clearly historical framing so a real audience is never misled.
